Paste an Excel formula and get it back formatted or flattened. Want it inside Excel? Install the add-in.

Tips

  • Set Separator to match your Excel: semicolon if your formulas look like IF(A1;1;0).
  • To see a formatted formula in Excel, expand the formula bar (Ctrl+Shift+U) after pasting it.
  • Formatting only changes spaces and line breaks. A check confirms the result is the same formula; if it isn't, nothing is shown.
  • Runs entirely in your browser: your formula is not sent anywhere. Privacy
