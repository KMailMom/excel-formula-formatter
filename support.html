Common questions

Format does nothing while I am typing in a cell

Excel blocks add-ins while a cell is in edit mode. Press Ctrl+Enter to confirm the edit (the cell stays selected), then press the shortcut.

Can I undo a change?

Yes. Format All and Flatten All ask for confirmation first, and every change can be undone with Ctrl+Z.

The keyboard shortcuts do not work

Another add-in or Excel itself may use the same combination. Change the shortcuts under Options > Settings.

The ribbon tab disappeared after an update

Close Excel and clear the Office add-in cache (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Microsoft\Office\16.0\Wef on Windows), then start Excel again.
